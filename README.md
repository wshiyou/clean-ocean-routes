# 🌊 Clean Ocean Routes

**Clean Ocean Routes** is a small browser game created for the **TUJ Fall 2025 Hackathon**.  
The goal of the project is to raise awareness about ocean pollution through an interactive and fun game.

---

## 🎮 About

Players control a small ship and collect floating plastic waste in 60 seconds.  
Every few levels, a big pollution “boss” appears that requires multiple hits to defeat.  
After each round, players can choose a perk to upgrade their ship — faster speed, more energy, or better firepower.  
It’s a simple game with a meaningful message: small actions can make a big difference.

## 🚀 How to Play
- Use **W, A, S, D** keys to move your ship.  
- **Left-click** to shoot pollution and defend yourself.  
- Collect the **yellow floating plastics** to earn points.  
- Avoid the **red storm circles**, they drain your energy and damage your ship.  
- Every 3 levels, a **Boss** appears — defeat it within 60 seconds!  
- After each round, choose one **perk** to upgrade your ship (more energy, faster fire, etc.).  
- If your damage reaches 100% or time runs out, the mission fails.
